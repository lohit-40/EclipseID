# Brag Plan: EclipseID

## What is this app?
EclipseID is a zero-knowledge credential verification system built on the Midnight Network that allows you to prove compliance (like KYC) without doxing your personal data.

## The angle
"Permissioned DeFi without the doxing." We lean into the brutalist design and the contrast between public blockchains (which expose everything) and Midnight Network (which protects data via ZK).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big brutalist type slamming in: "WEB3 WANTS YOU TO DOX YOURSELF." Followed immediately by: "WE DON'T."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The EclipseID 'Issue ZK Credential' card with the simulated Lace Wallet connect.
- A glowing 'ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PROOF GENERATED' system message.
- The Darkpool UI unlocking without exposing wallet address data.

## Outro / punchline
"Keep your identity off-chain. Enter the Darkpool."
EclipseID + x.com/EclipseID011.

## User flow worth showing
Connect Wallet → Issue ZK Credential → Access Darkpool Anonymously.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: brutalist, fast, unapologetic Web3 manifesto
- Interpretation: rapid pacing, huge typography, stark colors. Every line hits hard and holds just long enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 19 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #F2F0EB
- Accent: #FF4522
- Text: #1C1C1C
- Display font: Geist
- Body font: Geist
- Strongest visual element: The brutalist UI cards and heavy orange accent colors.

## Share copy (draft)
Verify your KYC without doxing your data. Zero-knowledge credentials on the Midnight Network are here. 🌙 #MidnightNetwork

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer
- Music: upbeat/chaotic (default track, if hyperframes has one)
- Music treatment: high energy, ducking for major SFX hits.
- Music cue guidance: detect at composition via `hyperframes beats`.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the #FF4522 orange glow or text scale pulse slightly.
- SFX posture: dense; motion-matched; hard impacts for text reveals, mechanical clicks for user flow.
- Audio-coupled moments: slam the text in on the beat. Card sounds for the ZK credential issuance.
- Restraint rule: Do not muddle the mix; keep impact SFX distinct from the music bed.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 3.5s
Huge black text `#1C1C1C` on `#F2F0EB` background.
Line 1 slams in: "WEB3 WANTS YOU TO DOX YOURSELF." (Hold 1.5s)
Screen flashes `#FF4522` orange.
Line 2 slams in: "WE DON'T." (Hold 1.5s)
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: aggressive, wake up the viewer
Audio-coupled idea: beat-aligned text slams with heavy thud SFX
Music: chaotic
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Solution — 3.5s
"EclipseID: Zero-Knowledge Credentials."
"On the Midnight Network."
Sequential/interaction: Line 1 appears, then Line 2 slides up from below.
Audio intent: tech-forward, revelatory
Audio-coupled idea: digital rising riser SFX
Transition mood: chaotic/zoom → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Flow (Issue) — 5.0s
A brutalist card appears: "ZK CREDENTIAL ISSUER".
A cursor clicks "CONNECT LACE".
A progress bar fills rapidly: "ISSUING PRIVATE CREDENTIAL... DONE".
Sequential/interaction: yes — card appears, cursor clicks, bar fills.
Audio intent: mechanical, satisfying completion
Audio-coupled idea: click SFX for the cursor, rapid digital ticks for the progress bar, chime for "DONE".
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Flow (Unlock) — 4.0s
Another card: "DARKPOOL OTC".
Cursor clicks "ACCESS ANONYMOUSLY".
A massive orange "ZK PROOF VERIFIED" stamp slams onto the screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor clicks, stamp slams.
Audio intent: powerful validation
Audio-coupled idea: heavy impact/stamp sound for "VERIFIED".
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.0s
"Keep your identity off-chain."
EclipseID Logo (or big text) + "@EclipseID011".
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: finality
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: fade to black
