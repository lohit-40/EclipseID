# Brag Plan: EclipseID - The 3D Cinematic Cut

## What is this app?
EclipseID is a zero-knowledge credential verification system built on the Midnight Network that allows you to prove compliance (like KYC) without doxing your personal data.

## The angle
"Privacy isn't a luxury; it's a right." A highly polished, 3D-animated, cinematic journey showing the contrast between the exposed, transparent Web3 world and the fully encrypted, private sanctuary of the Midnight Network.

## Hook (0:00 - 0:10)
A sweeping 3D camera move through a massive, transparent glass city (representing current Web3). Every data packet and wallet address is visible to everyone. The camera violently plunges into a dark, sleek, obsidian monolith (The Darkpool). 
Caption: "THEY CAN SEE EVERYTHING."
Caption: "UNTIL NOW."

## Key moments (The Middle 0:10 - 1:30)
- **The Issue Phase (0:10 - 0:40):** 3D close-ups of digital credentials locking into a cryptographic vault. Particles swirl as the ZK credential is minted. Sound of heavy mechanical gears locking.
- **The Shielding (0:40 - 1:10):** The Lace Wallet is connected. A visual "shield" or forcefield wraps around the user's data. A 3D holographic projection of the ZK Proof verifies successfully without revealing the underlying data.
- **The Darkpool (1:10 - 1:30):** The UI elements float in a 3D dark space. Trades and verifications happen in complete anonymity. 

## Outro / punchline (1:30 - 1:45)
Camera pulls out from the obsidian monolith to reveal the Midnight Network logo glowing against the dark void.
Caption: "EclipseID. Your Identity, Off-Chain."
Caption: "Enter the Darkpool."

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 105 seconds (1:45)

## Visual identity
- Background: #0A0A0A (Obsidian Black)
- Accent: #FF4522 (Neon Brutalist Orange)
- Text: #FFFFFF (Stark White)
- Display font: Space Grotesk
- Body font: JetBrains Mono
- Style: 3D rendered, cinematic, high contrast, smooth camera pans.

## Audio direction
- Role: Cinematic, escalating tension. 
- Music: Starts with a pulsing, tense synth bass (cyberpunk style). Escalates to a heavy, dropping electronic beat when entering the Darkpool.
- Music treatment: Heavy bass kicks. Audio ducking for the narrator/captions.
- SFX posture: 3D spatial audio. Glass shattering, heavy vault doors locking, digital scanning hums, and deep bass impacts for text reveals.
- Captions: High-contrast, dynamic subtitles that glitch into place with sound effects.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Glass City — 0:00 - 0:10
- **Visual:** 3D camera flies through transparent glass buildings. Data streams (wallets, balances) are floating in plain sight.
- **Caption:** "Web3 is fully transparent."
- **Caption:** "Maybe a little too transparent."
- **Audio:** Tense synth pad. Light digital chatter.

### Scene 2 — The Monolith — 0:10 - 0:30
- **Visual:** Camera dives into a pure black monolith. The glass shatters. We are now in a sleek, dark UI environment.
- **Caption:** "Welcome to the Darkpool."
- **Audio:** Heavy bass drop. Glass shatter SFX.

### Scene 3 — Minting Privacy — 0:30 - 1:00
- **Visual:** 3D rendering of the Lace Wallet. A glowing orange token represents the user's KYC. It gets encased in a metallic ZK vault. 
- **Caption:** "Zero-Knowledge Credentials."
- **Caption:** "Prove who you are."
- **Caption:** "Without showing your data."
- **Audio:** Mechanical locking SFX. Scanning lasers.

### Scene 4 — The Verification — 1:00 - 1:30
- **Visual:** 3D UI of the EclipseID Darkpool OTC. The ZK vault pings a verification node on the Midnight Network. A massive orange "ZK PROOF VERIFIED" stamp impacts the screen.
- **Caption:** "Permissioned DeFi."
- **Caption:** "Without the Doxing."
- **Audio:** Heavy impact/stamp sound. 

### Scene 5 — Outro — 1:30 - 1:45
- **Visual:** Smooth fade to black. The EclipseID and Midnight Network logos pulse in neon orange.
- **Caption:** "EclipseID. Built on Midnight."
- **Caption:** "x.com/EclipseID011"
- **Audio:** Reverb fading out. A final, satisfying digital chime.
